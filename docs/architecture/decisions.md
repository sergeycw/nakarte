# Technical decision register

One row per decision: what is selected, the short reason, and where the full reason is recorded. Do not expand the reason here: the details, numbers and rejected options are in the source. "Reason not recorded" means one of two things: the decision was made before OpenSpec, or its reason is not saved anywhere. Do not restore such a reason from memory. Record it again in a separate change.

Source notation: `design` is `openspec/changes/archive/<date>-<name>/design.md`, `spec` is `openspec/specs/<name>/spec.md`.
